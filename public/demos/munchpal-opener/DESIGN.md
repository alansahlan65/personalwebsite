# MunchPal opener

## Style prompt

Follow [the approved creative brief](../../../docs/munchpal-opener-creative-brief.md). Warm editorial staging makes the original mobile interface readable. Real food photographs, the original wordmark, matte UI planes, soft contact shadows, and one short conversation depth reveal carry the film. Lift, act, settle. Reading surfaces face the viewer. Two editorial cuts distinguish elapsed time and the separate donation example.

## Typography

Locally bundled Rubik Regular, Medium, Semibold, and Bold from the licensed variable font. Display claims use 76–108px, with a 64px floor. Source outlined typography remains in SVG exports. Timestamps retain source artwork where used. Critical UI controls are enlarged to at least 34px in the master.

## Spacing and motion

1920×1080 composition, 40 seconds, designed for 60fps. Keep important content within 96px. Staging padding follows the root DESIGN.md 4/8px scale. Authentic source UI spacing stays intact. Camera approaches take .65–.9s, followed by .15–.25s before taps. Source actions compress for .1s and release over .2s. No uncontrolled bounce. The first and final frames share the complete logo and headline composition.

## User-directed opening

The first frame contains the full original logo and “Food sharing, made clear.” Two original illustrated people then browse the authentic Food List. One scrolls a handset; the other selects a meal on the central screen. The portrait screen crops to its source food row and joins the tour at 7 seconds. These illustrations are later motion-study artwork. They do not represent research participants or a real completed pickup.

Use flat orange and green clothing, charcoal hair and shoes, and warm skin tones `#C88861` and `#E3A780`. Animate the heads, thumb and reaching arm around their joints. Keep the illustration face-on. Preserve the later UI sequence and reading holds.

## What not to do

- No invented dashboard, analytics, impact metrics, or instant acceptance.
- No replacement food photography or redrawn wordmark.
- No caption paragraphs, decorative badges, markers, or particle fields.
- No repeated scene fades. Use shared UI movement and the two specified chapter cuts.
- No audio, download CTA, or motion that prevents reading.
