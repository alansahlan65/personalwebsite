# Alan Sahlan portfolio

This portfolio presents Alan's product design practice and developing design engineering capabilities to prospective employers and collaborators.

## Language

**Product Designer**:
Alan's primary professional positioning and hiring focus, encompassing UI/UX and product design. His current employment title is UI/UX Designer.
_Avoid_: Treating Design Engineer as the primary hiring focus.

**Design Engineer**:
In this portfolio, a designer who can implement and refine interactive interfaces and connect product design intent with development through an understanding of technical feasibility and implementation choices. This is a role Alan is learning toward and open to, rather than a claim of an established employment title.
_Avoid_: Developer-only role, established Design Engineer employment history.

**Design-to-development bridge**:
The ability to translate design intent into actionable implementation guidance, explain technical constraints and trade-offs, and help designers and developers agree on how an interface should behave.
_Avoid_: Treating a visual handoff alone as proof of this capability.

**Design • Engineer**:
The exact hero phrase Alan has chosen to express the connection between design and engineering. It is identity copy, not a statement of his current employment title or a change to the Product Designer hiring priority.
_Avoid_: Rewriting it as Product Designer & Design Engineer, interpreting it as established Design Engineer employment.

**Later exploration**:
An interaction demonstration created after the original project to examine or communicate a design decision for the portfolio. It is separate from the original project contribution and does not establish past production use or testing.
_Avoid_: Original shipped feature, historical project outcome.
