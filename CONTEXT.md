# Alan Sahlan portfolio

This portfolio presents Alan's product design practice and developing design engineering capabilities to prospective employers and collaborators.

## Language

**Product Designer**:
Alan's primary professional positioning and hiring focus. His current employment title is UI/UX Designer.

**Design Engineer**:
A designer who implements and refines interfaces and bridges product design with development. Alan is developing this capability; it is not his established employment title.

**Design-to-development bridge**:
The practice of assessing feasibility, explaining constraints, and translating design intent into actionable implementation guidance and agreed interface behavior.

**Later exploration**:
A demonstration created after the original project to communicate a design decision. It is separate from the original contribution, production use, and historical testing.

**Portfolio motion study**:
The visible label for a later animated demonstration included in a case study.

**Prototype task attempt**:
One participant's attempt at one task in the Munchpal Figma evaluation. Counts describe prototype navigation, not live pickups or isolated revision effects.
