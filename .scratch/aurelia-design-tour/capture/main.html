How booking works

Before you make plans.

A few useful details about choosing your stay and what happens next.

Are my dates held while I plan?

Not yet. Dates are held only after availability, final pricing, and booking details are confirmed.

Are the nightly prices final?

The displayed rates are indicative, with IDR as the main reference and an approximate USD equivalent. Confirm the total for your dates, including any additional charges, before committing.

What is included in a stay?

Each stay lists its amenities and service details. Some services, such as a cook, may be available on request at an extra cost. Check the selected stay’s details and confirm inclusions for your dates.

What if I haven’t chosen an area or a house?

Leave your preferred area or stay open in the request form. You can compare the collection by holiday rhythm, guest capacity, and budget, or use Ask Aurelia to help narrow your choices.
