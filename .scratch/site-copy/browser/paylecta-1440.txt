Project
Client design project, June 2025
My role
Sole UI/UX designer
Scope
Responsive dashboards and visual system
One place to see the business

I put total balance and period summaries first, with gross and net revenue together for comparison. The desktop grid lets merchants scan several summaries at once; mobile follows the same order in one column.

Desktop dashboard
Mobile dashboard
Keep actions with the record

Search, filters and primary actions stay at the top of each screen. Customer rows keep their status and actions together, and the add-customer form opens over the list.

Customer records
Add a customer in context
See the invoice while making it

I placed the invoice preview beside the fields so merchants could check the amount and document while editing.

Invoice editor and preview
Invoice reminders on mobile
Mobile payouts
Make the payment state visible

Each payout has a written status: Success, Processing or Cancelled. Mobile cards keep the amount, status and next action together.

A shared visual language

I used Plus Jakarta Sans, orange accents and shared buttons, inputs and cards across the dashboards. Payment states keep consistent colors and labels on desktop and mobile.

Paylecta visual system
The design delivered

Desktop and mobile designs for monitoring finances, managing customers, creating invoices and reviewing payouts, supported by a shared visual system.
