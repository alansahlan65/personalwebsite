Two groups, two different jobs

Residents wanted to know what food was available before asking. Volunteers managed donations through paper records and WhatsApp. I designed separate flows for requesting food and coordinating bulk donations.

Research notes

I interviewed five Surabaya residents and five Garda Pangan volunteers, then mapped their needs and donation process into user flows, wireframes and a high-fidelity prototype.

Residents needed certainty before asking

All five residents described hesitation or embarrassment when asking for food. They wanted clear availability and pickup details, so I made each request start from a listing.

Volunteers needed a way to coordinate

I brought donation acceptance, pickup confirmation, reports and donation history into one workflow.

Keep bulk donations on their own path

Residents share food details and arrange pickups. Volunteers review bulk donations, confirm collection and record the pickup.
